1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Any3D ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Android app distributed through Google Play. It applies to the app itself. It does not apply to third-party websites or services you may open from the app, such as Google Play or the browser pages that host this policy.

Any3D is developed by 谢强. If you have questions, contact xq56798@gmail.com.

2. Information the app processes

Any3D is designed to work locally. We do not operate a backend that receives your photos, videos, screen content, device identifiers, or usage data.

2.1 Photos and videos you choose

When you use the offline conversion feature, you select media through Android's system photo picker. The app reads only the items you select. Selected media may be copied to app-private temporary storage for processing, and temporary working files are deleted when processing finishes. Converted images, videos, and GIFs are saved to your device's Pictures or Movies collection through Android MediaStore, and only when you choose to save them.

2.2 Screen content during 3D viewing

When you start 3D viewing, Android asks for your consent to capture screen content (MediaProjection). Captured frames are used only to render the 3D view on your device in real time. Any3D does not record, store, or transmit captured screen content. You can stop capture at any time from the app or from the Android notification. We recommend choosing "Share one app" in Android's screen-sharing prompt; if you choose "Share entire screen", the app may warn you or stop capture to avoid capturing its own overlay.

2.3 Settings, history, and purchase status

The app stores small amounts of data locally on your device, such as your settings, conversion history (file name, format, date, status, and output location), trial state, and whether Premium is unlocked. This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

3. What we do not collect

Through the app, we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or contacts;
  • your precise or approximate location;
  • your photos, videos, screen content, or files;
  • your device's advertising ID (AD_ID) or other advertising identifiers;
  • your app list, installed packages, or usage behavior;
  • your contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, microphone audio, or camera images;
  • crash logs, diagnostics, or analytics events.

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not show ads. It does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

4. Permissions and why we request them

  • Screen overlay (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): to display the 3D viewing panel above the app you select. We do not use it to read or interact with other apps' content.
  • Screen capture (MediaProjection): to render the selected app or screen in 3D in real time, only after your explicit consent.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the required foreground-service notification while screen capture or offline conversion is running. We do not send marketing push notifications.
  • Foreground service permissions: to keep screen capture and media conversion running while you use them.
  • Internet (INTERNET): used by Google Play Billing to query and restore purchases, and when you tap a link to open this policy or the Terms in your browser. The app does not upload your media or screen content.

Any3D does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, SMS, broad storage, or accessibility permissions. It does not use Android's AccessibilityService API and does not inject touches or keystrokes into other apps.

5. Payments and subscriptions

Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. Google Play handles your payment method and payment transaction. We do not receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment credentials.

To unlock and restore Premium features, the app reads product details, purchase status, and purchase tokens from Google Play on your device. This information is used locally and is not sent to us. Your purchase is associated with your Google Play account, not with an Any3D account.

Google's handling of payment and purchase information is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. To manage or cancel a subscription, use Google Play's subscriptions page.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through the app. We do not share data with advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers.

The only external service the app communicates with for its features is Google Play Billing, which processes payments as described above. The app may also open links in your browser, such as this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service, or Google Play. Those websites are operated by their respective providers and are governed by their own privacy policies. The app does not send your photos, videos, screen content, or app data to those websites.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain personal data on a server because we do not receive it. Data created by the app stays on your device until you delete it.

  • Delete conversion history entries, and optionally their output files, from the History screen.
  • Delete saved images, videos, or GIFs from your device's Photos or Files app.
  • Clear all app data in Android Settings > Apps > Any3D > Storage > Clear storage.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its private storage.

Because we do not maintain a server-side account or database, we cannot remotely access or delete data stored on your device. If you have a question about deletion, contact us at the email below.

Uninstalling the app does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

8. Security

Because processing happens on your device, your media and screen content are protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's security features. We do not transmit this content to a server. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but keeping processing local reduces the risk of data exposure in transit.

9. Children's privacy

Any3D is a general-purpose media and 3D viewing tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not transmit personal information to us, we do not maintain personal information about children or other users on a server. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Because we do not collect or maintain personal data on a server, we may not hold information that can be accessed or deleted in response to a request. You can delete locally stored data using the steps in Section 7. To make a request or ask a question, contact us at xq56798@gmail.com. We will respond as required by applicable law.

11. International users

Any3D processes data locally on your device. We do not transfer your photos, videos, screen content, or usage data internationally because we do not receive them. If you contact us by email, your email provider and ours may process the message in accordance with their policies.
